FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I manage our commercial alarm system from my smartphone?

Yes. Our commercial alarm installations feature intuitive mobile and web app access, allowing business owners and managers to arm, disarm, review entry logs, and receive instant intrusion push notifications from anywhere.

Does my Kansas City business need hardwired or wireless intrusion detection?

Hardwired sensors are recommended for drop-ceiling commercial spaces and newly constructed buildings due to maximum long-term reliability. However, we also deploy encrypted commercial wireless sensors for finished retail or historic spaces where running structured wire is impractical.

Can your burglar alarm system integrate with security cameras and card access?

Yes. USS specializes in complete low-voltage integration. We can tie your alarm system directly into commercial IP security cameras and access control readers for coordinated event triggers, automated lockdown, and synchronized video playback.
