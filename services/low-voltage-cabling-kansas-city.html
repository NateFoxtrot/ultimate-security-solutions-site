FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the difference between Cat6 and Cat6A for commercial spaces?

Cat6 supports up to 10 Gbps speeds at distances up to 165 feet and is ideal for standard office workstations, POS systems, and IP cameras. Cat6A maintains 10 Gbps speeds over full 328-foot runs and provides superior shielding against crosstalk, making it the preferred choice for dense server rooms, heavy data environments, and future-proof enterprise networks.

Can you install network cabling in an active office or retail store?

Yes. We regularly work in operational commercial spaces across the Kansas City metro. We can schedule pulls, terminations, and testing during after-hours or phased windows to minimize noise and ensure zero disruption to your daily business operations.

Do you test and certify each network drop after installation?

Every drop is continuity-tested, wiremap-verified, and certified for gigabit throughput. We supply clear labeling on patch panels and wall jacks so your internal IT staff or MSP can manage the network seamlessly.
