FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What cable standards and certifications do you support for MDF/IDF builds?

We install and certify Cat6, Cat6A (UTP/STP), single-mode, and multi-mode (OM3/OM4) fiber optic cabling adhering to TIA/EIA-568 standards, providing detailed wiremap, attenuation, and continuity test results for all terminated drops.

Can USS clean up and re-patch an existing, cluttered server room or IDF closet?

Yes. We specialize in live and maintenance-window closet remediations, including tracing lines, swapping tangled patch leads for custom zero-slack cables, reorganizing patch panels, and installing proper vertical/horizontal wire management.

Do you handle ladder rack, cable tray, and server rack installations?

Yes. We assemble, floor-anchor, and wall-brace 2-post and 4-post open frame racks, secure server enclosures, wall-mount swing cabinets, overhead runway ladder racks, and basket trays with proper grounding and bonding.
