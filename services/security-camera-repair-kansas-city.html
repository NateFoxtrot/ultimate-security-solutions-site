FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why is my security camera feed showing offline or video loss?

Video loss is typically caused by power supply failure, a damaged Ethernet/coaxial cable run, a failed PoE switch port, or an IP address conflict. Our technicians trace the line with digital cable analyzers and diagnostic tools to quickly identify and repair the exact break point.

Can you repair my NVR if it has stopped recording or beeping continuously?

Yes. Continuous beeping and recording stoppage usually indicate a degraded or failed surveillance hard drive, thermal throttling, or corrupted storage partitions. We test drive health on site, replace failed drives with dedicated surveillance-rated storage, and restore scheduled recording.

Do you repair security camera systems installed by other companies?

Yes. We regularly take over, troubleshoot, and maintain existing camera systems installed by other contractors or legacy providers across Kansas City, regardless of equipment brand.
