Our 4-Step Deployment & Installation Process

We follow a disciplined four-stage implementation process designed to minimize business downtime. Stage 1 begins with a comprehensive on-site RF spectrum analysis and architectural blueprint review to identify structural attenuation factors, high-density client zones, and optimal cable pathways.



Stage 2 involves physical infrastructure deployment. Our technicians pull dedicated Cat6/Cat6A data lines from your MDF/IDF server racks, terminate patch panels, mount heavy-duty enterprise access point brackets at specified heights and angles, and install managed PoE switching gear.



Stages 3 and 4 encompass network configuration, post-installation validation heat-mapping, and client handoff. We tune channel allocations and transmit power levels to prevent co-channel interference, verify throughput across all coverage zones, and provide your IT team with comprehensive network documentation and administrative credentials.
