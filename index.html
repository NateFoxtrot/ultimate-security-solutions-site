Structured Cabling

Our Process

Every cabling project starts with a comprehensive site survey and path analysis. We map cable routes, identify IDF/MDF locations, calculate load requirements, and specify materials before pulling a single foot of cable. Our installations comply with TIA-568 standards — proper bend radius, fire stopping, cable management, and labeling on every run. We test with Fluke DSX-series analyzers and deliver documented certification results.

Why USS

23 years in the field. We've wired hospitals, data centers, multi-story office buildings, industrial facilities, retail chains, and government buildings across the Kansas City metro and beyond. We're product-agnostic — we install what your project specifies, sourced through distribution partners like ADI and SES for competitive pricing and availability. We show up on time, label everything, test every run, and deliver clean as-built documentation.

What We Install

Copper (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6A) and fiber optic (single-mode, multi-mode, OM3/OM4/OM5) with LC, SC, and MTP terminations. Full cable infrastructure: patch panels, cable trays, J-hooks, conduit, rack-and-stack, grounding and bonding. Backbone and horizontal distribution for new construction, renovations, and multi-site rollouts.

Access Control

Our Process

We design, install, and configure electronic access control systems that let you manage who enters your facility, when, and through which doors. From single-door standalone readers to multi-site cloud-managed platforms with hundreds of doors, we engineer around your operational requirements, compliance needs, and growth plans.

Why USS

23 years of experience integrating access control with buildings, IT networks, surveillance systems, and security policies. We handle the full picture — door hardware, locking systems, wiring infrastructure, controller programming, credential enrollment, and system integration. Product-agnostic with access to every major manufacturer through ADI and SES distribution.

What We Install

Card readers, fob systems, mobile credentials, biometric readers, keypad entry, electromagnetic locks, electric strikes, panic hardware, door position sensors, and request-to-exit devices. Cloud-managed and on-premise platforms. Integration with video surveillance for event-triggered recording and visual verification.
